Privacy Policy

Pet Fighters - Arena Battles
Also referred to in the game as Pet Fighters.
Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Ariel Yabetz, an individual developer based in Israel, handles information for this game. The game may appear under the developer names Ariely Fighters or ArielY. For privacy questions and requests, contact arielyfighters@gmail.com.

You can play the offline modes without online sign-in. Optional multiplayer uses Epic Online Services (EOS) for guest sign-in, rooms and connections between players. The current version has no advertising or in-app purchases.

1. Offline play and local storage

Campaign, Battle, Survival and Training do not initiate internet connections. Progress, settings and Survival records are stored on your device in files such as progress.cfg, settings.cfg and survival.cfg. The game does not upload these files to the developer.

On Android, use the system's Clear storage option or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. On Windows, saves are in your user profile, normally under %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Pet Fighters. Deleting the game installation folder does not remove this separate data folder. Local removal does not delete records stored by online-service providers.

2. Optional multiplayer

When you choose Multiplayer and continue through the online notice, the game uses Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games. This involves:

  • Guest identifiers: EOS creates a Device ID and a guest player identifier. You do not sign in with an Epic account. When creating the Device ID, the current game supplies a generic label, “Pet Fighters device”, rather than your actual device model. Earlier test versions supplied the device model at that point. EOS stores guest sign-in credentials locally; removing game data may not remove all of those credentials, particularly on Windows.
  • Nickname: you choose a session nickname or use a generated guest name. The game does not save this nickname between app sessions. It is shared with the other player. Public-room host nicknames are also included in the room data held by EOS.
  • Room information: EOS handles room codes, the host nickname for public rooms, arena, level, rules, visibility, availability and game/content version. Private rooms are hidden from the game's public lists, but room details can still be found through a room code or a direct search of the room service. Do not put sensitive information in a nickname.
  • Connections and match data: the players exchange inputs, match state, fighter and arena choices and readiness information. The game tries a direct peer-to-peer connection first. Each player's IP address can be visible to the other player's device and to Epic. If needed, Epic relays the connection. IP addresses may reveal approximate location; the game does not request GPS or precise location.
  • Service diagnostics: the EOS SDK sends Epic service-quality information, including a random session identifier, API request counts, response times and success or error codes.

The developer can access records for this game's online services through Epic's developer portal, including guest player identifiers and room activity. The game does not maintain a separate developer-operated player database.

3. Purposes and recipients

We use information to provide the multiplayer features you request, connect players, maintain the online service, answer support requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Information goes to Epic and its service providers for online services, to other players' devices as described above, and to Google when you contact our Gmail support address.

For the game-service data handled on our behalf, we determine the purposes of processing and use Epic as a service provider. Epic may also process service-quality and other information for its own purposes under its applicable terms. Epic and its providers may process information in the United States and other countries. See Epic's service-provider list. Epic's general privacy policy describes Epic's own practices; it does not establish a retention schedule for every EOS record associated with this game.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the online functionality you request to the extent necessary to perform that service, legitimate interests in operating and supporting the game, and applicable legal obligations. Accepting game terms does not itself constitute privacy consent. If a feature requires separate consent, it must be obtained separately.

4. Features the current version does not use

The current game has no advertising SDK, advertising-ID integration, in-app purchases, voice chat, contacts access, camera or microphone access, separate gameplay analytics feature or automatic crash-report upload. EOS service-quality measurements are described above. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Advertising and purchases are planned features, not descriptions of the current version. This policy will be updated before introducing features that change data handling.

5. Support and diagnostic logs

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Our mailbox uses Gmail, provided by Google; see Google's privacy policy. Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information.

The game writes diagnostic logs locally: text files in the Windows game-data folder and system logs on Android. Logs can contain room codes, shortened online identifiers, connection information and errors. Debug/test builds can also log the device model, locale and local network addresses. The game does not automatically upload these logs. If you send logs for support, they become part of your support request.

6. Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until you remove them. Session nicknames are not saved by the game between app sessions; this does not mean that all associated service logs are immediately deleted. Room data is used while rooms operate. We have not confirmed a fixed retention period for Epic's guest-identity records, room records or operational logs, and do not promise automatic or immediate deletion of those records.

We delete support emails and attachments within 12 months after resolving the request, unless a legal obligation or ongoing dispute requires longer retention. This deletion is handled manually.

To request access, correction or deletion, email arielyfighters@gmail.com. For online records, include your Online support ID, available after connecting through Multiplayer under Settings → Licenses → Terms of Use & Privacy → Online privacy. Share it privately. The ID alone is not proof of identity; we may need proportionate verification before disclosing or deleting information.

Epic provides developer tools for requesting export or deletion of player data held on a developer's behalf. We have not confirmed the full coverage of those tools for this game's Device ID guests. We will investigate your request and explain what we can do and any limitations. We cannot promise deletion of every Epic or subprocessor log. Removing local files does not delete server records. We can handle support-mail requests directly; we cannot remotely read or remove files on your device.

7. Your choices and rights

You are not legally required to provide personal information to play. You can use the offline modes without Multiplayer. Online play cannot work without the guest identifiers, connection information and game data described above. You can change your nickname for a later session.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, object to processing, restrict processing, or withdraw consent where processing actually relies on consent. Contact us to exercise those rights. You may also complain to your relevant data-protection authority. These rights can be subject to legal exceptions and verification requirements.

8. Children and teenagers

The intended audience is teenagers aged 13 and over and adults; the game is not intended for children under 13. The game does not currently ask for age. If you believe a child under 13 has used online play, contact us. We will address the report, explain local data removal and investigate available online-record deletion options. We do not promise that deleting local data deletes Epic's records.

9. This website and platform services

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses and technical request information, to deliver and secure the page. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or forms to this page. See GitHub's privacy statement. Google Play and other distribution platforms separately handle information for their own platform services under their policies.

10. Updates and contact

We will publish changes on this page with an updated effective date, and update the in-game notice when the game's data practices change. Privacy contact: arielyfighters@gmail.com.
